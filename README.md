A make.fun entry for the **Funniest Lost Sock Status Update** bounty: one status update of 120 characters at most in the voice of a lost sock.

## The entry

> Day 47 behind the dryer. Started a family with a dust bunny. Tell my pair I moved on. He's welcome at the wedding.

(114 characters. The sock isn't eaten by the dryer — it's living behind it, 47 days in, and has moved on: started a family with a dust bunny and dumped its pair, who is cheerfully invited to the wedding.)

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with in-code gate checks: single line, non-blank, 1–120 characters, plain ASCII, plus a read-back verification that the file on disk is byte-identical to the entry.
